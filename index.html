❓ FAQ

How do I add an expense?
Tap the "+" button and enter the amount, category, and optional note.

How does receipt scanning work?
Use the camera to scan receipts. Lumiere automatically detects the total amount and saves time.

Can I edit or delete an expense?
Yes, tap on any transaction to edit or delete it anytime.

How are my expenses organized?
Expenses are grouped by date and category, making it easy to track your spending habits.

Is my data secure?
Yes, your data is securely stored and handled according to our privacy policy.

Do I need an account?
No, you can start using Lumiere immediately without creating an account.

Which currencies are supported?
Lumiere supports multiple currencies including EUR, USD, and VND.

Can I use the app offline?
Yes, basic features like adding and viewing expenses work offline.
